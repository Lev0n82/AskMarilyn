## Selected Approach: Mid-Century Modern / Editorial Print

I have selected the **Mid-Century Modern / Editorial Print** approach. This style perfectly complements the persona of Marilyn vos Savant—intelligent, elegant, and clear. It transforms the dry subject of software testing into something approachable and sophisticated, much like her "Ask Marilyn" column does for complex logic puzzles. The focus on typography and readability ensures that the educational content is the star, while the warm, structured design provides a comfortable learning environment.

**Design Philosophy**:
"We are building a digital editorial experience that honors intelligence and clarity. Our design will be structured yet warm, authoritative yet accessible, using typography and layout to guide the learner through the logic of Action-Based Testing with the elegance of a well-written column."
