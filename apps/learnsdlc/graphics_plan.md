## Visual Style Guidelines
- Mid-century modern illustration style
- Color palette: Navy blue (#1a365d), Coral/salmon (#e07a5f), Cream/beige background (#f5f0e6)
- Geometric shapes with organic curves
- Vintage 1950s-60s aesthetic
- Typography: Bold, sans-serif with slight retro feel
- Clever/witty visual metaphors
- Can be humorous, satirical, or slightly dark/horrifying when appropriate
